### §5 Circadian timing gates
- **Target:** 900–1,100 words
- **Subsections:** 5.1 XPA/NER (central node) → 5.2 OCT2/SLC22A2 → 5.3 GSH/redox rhythms → 5.4 Mucosal proliferation → 5.5 BMAL1/ARNTL paradox (**featured as article highlight**) → 5.6 PER/CRY and clock integrity
- **BMAL1 paradox approach:** Explicitly state that BMAL1 simultaneously (a) facilitates renal injury in wild-type models [Zha 2020], (b) acts as a tumor suppressor in OSCC [Li 2022], and (c) when silenced promotes cisplatin resistance in NPC [Peng 2019]. Conclude: "This tissue-specific duality underscores that clock-based toxicity modulation is not unidirectional, and that a naive 'restore the clock → reduce toxicity' framework is insufficient."
- **Key references:** Sancar 2015/2021, Kang 2009/2010, Yang 2018, Oda 2014, Zha 2020, Peng 2019, Li 2022, Li 1997, Lee & Sancar 2011a/b, Bjarnason 2001, Gu 2021, Yang 2021, Sassa 2010, Okyar 2011, Okabe 2014, Nakagawa 2008, Kramer 2022
