## Key chronotherapy-logistical insight (for Section 3)
RT delivery calendar constrains cisplatin scheduling. Cisplatin given on days 1, 22, 43 (q3-weekly) or each weekday (weekly) — dates determined by RT calendar, not circadian phase or patient chronotype. Pharmacy prep, IV chair, nursing schedules, patient transport all compress administration into morning-hours slots. No current trial protocol records or controls for time-of-day. This is the core rationale for Section 3 ("biological-time blind").
